## 공통 (매번 맨 앞에)

```text
Create a 3D digital sculpt of a muscular male bodybuilder figure, rendered as a single-color matte light-gray clay model (ZBrush-style clay render) on a pure white (#FFFFFF) background. Soft studio lighting from the upper front. No cast shadow on the floor.

This image is a base for a muscle-group diagram, so the body must read as 14 clearly separated blocks. Sculpt a deep, clean, continuous groove along the border of every block listed below, like the seams between the panels of an action figure. Inside each block keep the surface smooth and simple: no striations, no veins, no small sub-muscle divisions, no skin wrinkles.

The 14 blocks (left and right sides symmetric):
1. Shoulder: the whole deltoid cap as ONE block.
2. Chest: the whole pectoral as ONE block.
3. Biceps: the front of the upper arm as ONE block.
4. Triceps: the back of the upper arm as ONE block.
5. Forearm: from elbow to wrist as ONE block.
6. Abdomen: abs and side obliques together as ONE block. The six-pack may be faintly hinted, but the outer border of the whole abdomen must be the strong line.
7. Trapezius: from the back of the neck across the upper back, narrowing down to a point at mid-back, as ONE kite-shaped block.
8. Lats: the wide back wings from the armpit down to the waist as ONE block.
9. Lower back: the two vertical columns beside the spine, between the lats and the glutes, as ONE block.
10. Glutes: the whole buttock as ONE block.
11. Quadriceps: the front and outer thigh as ONE block.
12. Hamstrings: the back of the thigh as ONE block.
13. Adductors: the inner thigh as ONE block.
14. Lower leg: calf and shin together as ONE block.

The borders that matter most: shoulder/chest, shoulder/upper arm, biceps/triceps, upper arm/forearm, chest/abdomen, abdomen/lats, trapezius/shoulder, trapezius/lats, lats/lower back, lower back/glutes, glutes/hamstrings, quadriceps/adductors, quadriceps/hamstrings, thigh/lower leg.

Neck, head, hands, feet, knees and elbows are plain neutral shapes, not muscle blocks. Bald mannequin head with minimal facial features.

Strictly avoid: nipples, genitals (smooth mannequin-like groin), clothing, text, labels, arrows, any color other than gray, logos, watermark, extra limbs.
```

## 그림별 (공통 뒤에 하나만)

### 01 전신 앞
```text
View: full body, front view, camera straight-on at waist height with a long lens (almost no perspective distortion).
Pose: standing upright and relaxed, perfectly symmetric. Arms held away from the torso by about 30 degrees so there is a clear gap between each arm and the body, elbows almost straight, palms facing the thighs. Feet shoulder-width apart, toes forward, legs not touching.
Framing: the whole figure from the top of the head to the toes, centered, with a small even margin. Portrait 9:16.
```

### 02 전신 뒤
```text
View: exactly the same figure, same pose, same proportions and same framing as the front view, but seen directly from behind. The silhouette should be the mirror image of the front view.
Pose: standing upright and relaxed, perfectly symmetric. Arms held away from the torso by about 30 degrees with a clear gap between each arm and the body, elbows almost straight. Feet shoulder-width apart, legs not touching.
Framing: the whole figure from the top of the head to the heels, centered, with a small even margin. Portrait 9:16.
```

### 03 가슴 확대 (45°)
```text
View: close-up of the chest, camera rotated 45 degrees toward the figure's left side, at chest height.
Pose: arms relaxed and hanging down, slightly away from the torso, not covering the chest or abdomen.
Framing: from the chin down to the navel, both shoulders inside the frame. Square 1:1.
Must be clearly visible: chest, both shoulders, upper abdomen, the top of the biceps.
```

### 04 팔 ~ 어깨 확대
```text
View: close-up of the figure's left arm and shoulder, three-quarter view from the front-left, at shoulder height.
Pose: the left arm hanging down, held away from the torso by about 30 degrees, elbow slightly bent, palm facing the thigh. The arm must not cross in front of the torso.
Framing: from the side of the neck down to the wrist, the whole arm inside the frame. Square 1:1.
Must be clearly visible: shoulder, biceps, the outer edge of the triceps, forearm, and the border between each of them.
```

### 05 굽힌 다리 확대
```text
View: close-up of the lower body seen from the figure's right side, slightly from behind, camera at hip height.
Pose: the right leg (near the camera) lifted like a high-knee step, hip and knee both bent about 90 degrees. The left leg standing straight. Arms raised out of the frame.
Framing: from the waist down to the middle of the calves. Square 1:1.
Must be clearly visible: glutes, quadriceps, hamstrings, adductors of the standing leg, lower leg, and the border between each of them.
```

### 06 등 확대
```text
View: close-up of the back seen directly from behind, camera at mid-back height.
Pose: standing upright, arms hanging down and held slightly away from the torso.
Framing: from the back of the head down to the top of the glutes, both arms down to the elbows inside the frame. Square 1:1.
Must be clearly visible: trapezius (kite shape), both shoulders, lats, lower back columns, triceps, and the border between each of them.
```
